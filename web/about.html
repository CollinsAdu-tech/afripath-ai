The vision

Where we are going

AfriPath AI is the first step. It is the MVP of something bigger, and it will grow in stages. We are not promising dates, only direction.

Live now01

AfriPath AI

Students take a survey about their interests, strengths, ways of working, background and goals. The survey feeds our recommendation engine, which matches them to career paths, explains every match, and builds a roadmap from real courses and projects. Today the engine covers a first set of career paths.

Next02

More career fields

We will keep adding career paths to the engine, so more students find a field that fits them, in areas well beyond technology.

Next03

More opportunities

Scholarships, internships, summer programmes, certifications, communities and grants, each one labelled with how far it has been checked, so students know what to trust.

Next04

More live mentorship

Connecting students with mentors and with organisations that offer mentorship, so a path on paper becomes someone to walk it with.

How we build it

Explained, not a black box

Every match shows the answers behind it and how the score was worked out. The score is a guide for exploring, never a prediction of success.

Honest about what we know

Courses and opportunities come from a source list we have not independently verified, and we say so. We never call something free or open unless the source does.

Private by default

No account is needed and the survey asks for no name, email or phone number. The engine does not store your answers.
