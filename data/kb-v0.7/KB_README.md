## Important data-quality and safety notes
- This is structured knowledge-base data, not a trained model or a finished recommendation engine.
- v0.7 additions were structured from user-provided roadmap notes. The exact source subpage URLs were not supplied; the TAP Paths root is retained as a source pointer.
- Structural validation checks ID uniqueness and roadmap-to-resource/career references. It does not confirm that external URLs work or that courses, cohorts, certifications, scholarships, stipends, or hackathons are currently available.
- A few resources are deliberately marked as leads where an exact URL or current listing was not confirmed. Blank URLs mean no specific URL was supplied; do not fabricate one.
- Quantum random-number, BB84-style, teleportation, Grover, and variational-circuit projects are educational simulations; they are not production security implementations or proof of practical quantum advantage.
- AI Safety includes technical alignment, evaluation, governance, and responsible-AI practice. These are related but distinct areas; recommendations should be clear about which focus they cover.
- Fellowship funding, sponsored travel, stipend details, program eligibility, and dates are unverified and must be checked on official pages.
- Project-management course access, free-tier limits, and certification/exam costs can change. Practice projects before paying for credentials.
- Earlier v0.6 cautions remain: AWS Free Tier can incur charges; apply budgets/alerts, MFA, and least-privilege access. The MLH link in Full Stack Web Development references the 2024 season; verify current opportunities and Student Developer Pack terms.
- Resource verification statuses must be updated before the app labels an item “verified,” “free,” “open,” or “funded.”

## Recommendation-engine integration note
Use deterministic, explainable matching for MVP V1. Do not treat this archive as model weights. Filter by prerequisites and student constraints, score matches using the student’s stated interests, strengths, academic preparation, time, budget, internet access, and local availability, and return reasons plus data-quality notes. Never imply that an opportunity is open or funded unless its status has been verified.
